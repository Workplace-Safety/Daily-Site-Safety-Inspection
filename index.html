1. Site Access & Housekeeping

Entrance and exit routes are clear and accessible
Work areas are clean and free of debris
Materials are properly stored and stacked
Trash and waste properly disposed in designated areas
Walking and working surfaces are free of trip hazards

2. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

All workers wearing required hard hats
Safety glasses/eye protection worn as required
High-visibility vests/clothing worn as required
Appropriate work boots/safety footwear
Gloves appropriate for task being performed
Fall protection harness/lanyard (if working at heights)

3. Fall Protection & Working at Heights

Guardrails installed at all open edges 6 ft or higher
Ladders secured and in good condition
Scaffolds properly erected with toe boards and rails
Openings and holes are covered or barricaded

4. Electrical Safety

GFCI protection in place for temporary power
Extension cords in good condition (no cuts or damage)
Electrical panels accessible and properly labeled
No exposed wiring or energized parts

5. Tools & Equipment

Power tools have required guards in place
Equipment inspected before use (visual check)
Damaged or defective tools tagged out and removed
Heavy equipment operators are competent/certified
⚠ STOP WORK if any critical safety hazard is identified. Do not proceed until hazard is corrected.
